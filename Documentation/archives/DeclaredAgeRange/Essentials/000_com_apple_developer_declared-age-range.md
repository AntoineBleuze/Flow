Type
Boolean
Default: true
This entitlement works with the Declared Age Range framework, which enables people to share
their age range with your app.
To use Declared Age Range, add this entitlement to your app by enabling the Declared Age Range
capability on your target in Xcode. For more information, refer to Adding capabilities to your app.
